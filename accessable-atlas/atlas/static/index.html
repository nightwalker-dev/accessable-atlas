Permissions, Privacy & Optional Integrations

AccessAble Atlas enforces strict server-side ownership, workspace boundaries, and privacy-by-default data handling before requesting any optional capability:

  • Photo Evidence Consent & EXIF Scrubbing: Every uploaded photo is validated by binary magic bytes, stripped of GPS/camera EXIF metadata via server-side re-encoding, and held in private storage until consent is verified.
  • Optional Accessibility Briefing (Non-Blocking): The directory works 100% without AI. Synthesized route briefings require your explicit consent toggle below.
  • Privacy-Minimized Operational Telemetry (Tinybird): Only aggregate counts and freshness buckets are recorded when opted in—never raw text, emails, or photos.
